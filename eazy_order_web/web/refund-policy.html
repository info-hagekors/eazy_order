Refund & Cancellation Policy

At eazyOrder, we act as a technology platform enabling customers to place food and beverage orders digitally at participating cafes and restaurants. Since food is prepared fresh based on each order, we do not allow cancellations or refunds once an order has been placed.

Refund Eligibility

  • The cafe/restaurant is unable to fulfill the order.
  • You were charged but the order was not received due to a technical failure.
  • The item received was incorrect or undelivered.

In such cases, complaints must be raised within 2 hours of order placement.

How to Request a Refund

Email us at info@hagekors.com with:

  • Your Order ID
  • Name of the restaurant
  • Description of the issue

If eligible, refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days via Razorpay.

Non-Refundable Scenarios

  • Change of mind after placing the order
  • Complaints made after food is consumed
  • Restaurant delivery delays unless explicitly cancelled
